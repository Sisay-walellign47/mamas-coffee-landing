Rooted in Origin

Born Along the Nile, Roasted for Your Morning

Founded in 2021, Nile Coffee began with a simple philosophy: honor the birthplace of coffee by partnering directly with smallholder farming cooperatives along the Blue and White Nile river basins.

We pay 45% above Fair Trade baselines, guaranteeing regenerative farming and empowering multi-generational coffee families.
